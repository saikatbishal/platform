Platform · map spike

▼

Real geometry, no map library, no tiles. 36 states, 453 rail lines, 8,696 stations, 214 cities — 248 KB of path data, all of it in memory.

Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to zoom in. Detail appears as you go in: trunk lines first, then branches; twelve cities, then ninety, then all of them.

Untick "Follow the track" to see the bug: the journey becomes a 7-point straight line that cuts 13% of its length through the Bay of Bengal. Ticked, it runs through all 338 stops the trains actually call at — 0% over water, and 2,294 km instead of a 2,024 km guess.

District borders build on first use, not on first paint — watch the districts row in the readout cross zoom 4×. In the real app that's where the lazy fetch goes.

Pan and zoom are one transform, so nothing is fetched mid-gesture and nothing can stutter. That's the whole argument for skipping tiles.
